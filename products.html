Our Coffee and Tea Products

Our Coffee Selections

Our coffees are roasted in small batches to ensure optimal freshness, bold flavors, and exquisite aromas.

  • Arabica Coffee: Our premium Arabica coffee is carefully selected from high-quality coffee-growing regions and has a rich and smooth flavour.
  • Dark Roast Espresso: Features a bold, intense profile with notes of dark chocolate and caramel for a powerful morning boost.
  • Vanilla Hazelnut Blend: A smooth medium roast infused with natural vanilla and roasted hazelnut undertones.

Our Tea Selections

Hand-picked from lush, mist-covered hills, our teas offer soothing relaxation and vibrant taste profiles.

  • Kenyan Black Tea: Our Kenyan Black Tea provides a rich aroma and refreshing taste.
  • Organic Green Tea: Light, crisp, and packed with antioxidants for a clean and revitalizing sip.
  • Earl Grey Supreme: Classic black tea scented with fragrant oil of bergamot and delicate cornflower petals.
